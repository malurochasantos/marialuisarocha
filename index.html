About

I am a PhD candidate at Brown University studying how atmospheric processes shape hazardous weather in the Arctic. My research brings together numerical weather modeling, atmospheric dynamics, and questions about maritime safety. I am particularly interested in understanding why polar lows intensify and how better weather information can support decisions in high-latitude environments.

This website is a starting point for sharing my research, publications, and professional activities.

Research

Polar lows and Arctic storms

Understanding the development and intensity of compact Arctic cyclones through atmospheric modeling and dynamical analysis.

Marine cold-air outbreaks

Studying how cold-air outbreaks and air–sea heat exchanges influence polar low development.

Sea ice and maritime hazards

Connecting Arctic weather and sea-ice research with risks relevant to marine operations.
